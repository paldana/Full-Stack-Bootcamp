About Me

I’m a Senior Software Engineer with nine years of experience building full-stack applications, spanning both fast-paced aerospace & defense R&D and high-scale enterprise fintech. Throughout my career, I’ve specialized in operating at the intersection of frontend usability and robust backend architecture, combining low-level optimization with highly scalable distributed systems.

I rapidly adapted to their massive cloud-native architecture and eventually stepped up as an Acting Product Lead. My focus there was entirely on operational excellence: I architected a multi-channel notification orchestration engine that reduced repeat customer complaints by 87%, built Python automation suites that accelerated testing cycles by 9x, and partnered on AI-augmented layers that reduced workflow times by 78%. I also served as a Security Champion to enforce secure coding and OWASP compliance across different teams.
